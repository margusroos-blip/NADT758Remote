NAD Remote Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NAD Remote app handles data when you use the app.

Summary

  • The app does not require account sign-in.
  • The app does not sell personal data.
  • The app does not send your personal data to the developer's own servers.

Data the app processes

To control your receiver on your local network, the app processes:

  • Your receiver's local IP address (for connection).
  • Network discovery responses from your local network (for finding compatible devices).
  • Receiver playback and control state (for showing and controlling current status).
  • App preferences such as language, theme, and button layout.

Where data is stored

App preferences are stored locally on your device using Android storage mechanisms. The app communicates directly with your receiver over your local network.

Data sharing

The app does not share your personal data with third parties for advertising or sale.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the app listing support contact.
